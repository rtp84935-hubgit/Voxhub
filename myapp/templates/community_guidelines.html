✦ THE VOXHUB COMMUNITY

Our Community,
Our Responsibility.

VoxHub is a place to share your experiences, express your ideas, discover people, and build meaningful connections. These guidelines help make our community safer, more respectful, and welcoming for everyone.

🤝 Respect Everyone 🛡️ Protect Privacy 💜 Share Responsibly

🤝

1. Treat Everyone with Respect

A healthy community begins with how we treat one another.

  • Be respectful when expressing opinions, even when others disagree with you.
  • No harassment or bullying. Do not threaten, intimidate, repeatedly target, or humiliate other users.
  • No hate speech. Do not attack or dehumanize people based on protected characteristics.
  • Respect different experiences, backgrounds, and perspectives.
  • Do not encourage others to harass, threaten, or target another person.
📸

2. Share Content Responsibly

Think about the impact of what you post, comment on, and share.

  • Share content that you have the right to upload or distribute.
  • No harmful threats or encouragement of violence.
  • Do not upload sexually exploitative content or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Do not publish content intended to scam, defraud, or deliberately deceive other users.
  • Do not share malicious links, phishing attempts, or content designed to compromise another person's account or device.
  • Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent your identity to deceive others.
  • Provide context when sharing sensitive or potentially misleading information.
Remember: Public visibility does not automatically give you permission to reuse someone else's private content, personal information, or copyrighted work.
🔒

3. Protect Privacy and Personal Information

Everyone deserves control over their personal information.

  • Never publish another person's home address, phone number, passwords, private messages, or other sensitive information without proper authorization.
  • Do not use VoxHub to stalk, threaten, or monitor someone without their consent.
  • Keep your login credentials private and use a strong, unique password.
  • Do not create fake accounts to evade restrictions or target other users.
  • If you discover a privacy or security issue, report it through the appropriate channel rather than exploiting it.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

4. Face Detection and Consent

Our face-related features must be used responsibly.

  • Use face detection features only for their intended purpose.
  • Do not use these features to harass, threaten, stalk, or embarrass another person.
  • Respect people's privacy when uploading images or videos containing their faces.
  • Respond honestly to requests concerning your appearance in uploaded content.
  • Do not attempt to bypass face-detection safeguards or misuse information produced by the system.
  • Report incorrect face detections or suspected misuse so they can be reviewed.
Important: Automated face detection can make mistakes. A detection result should not, by itself, be treated as proof of identity, consent, or wrongdoing.
🤖

5. AI Moderation and Automated Decisions

AI can help identify potentially harmful content, but it is not perfect.

  • Comments and other supported content may be checked by automated moderation systems.
  • Do not deliberately attempt to evade moderation by disguising threats, harassment, or prohibited content.
  • Automated systems may misunderstand context, sarcasm, language, or intent.
  • If you believe content was incorrectly flagged or an action was taken by mistake, contact the platform administrator through the available support process.
  • Do not treat an AI-generated result as a definitive judgment about a person's character or intentions.
Our approach: AI moderation should support a safer community. Where appropriate and available, reports and disputed moderation decisions should receive additional review.
💬

6. Follow, Comment, and Message Responsibly

Make every interaction respectful and intentional.

  • Keep comments relevant and avoid repetitive, disruptive messages.
  • Respect follow requests and other users' decisions about who they interact with.
  • Do not send threatening, abusive, or unwanted messages repeatedly.
  • Do not use fake engagement, spam, or coordinated activity to manipulate the community.
  • Respect blocks, restrictions, and other available privacy controls.
  • Do not exploit bugs or platform features to disrupt other users' experiences.
🚩

7. Report Problems Honestly

Reports help the community identify content that may violate these guidelines.

  • Report content or behavior that you genuinely believe violates these guidelines.
  • Provide an accurate reason and relevant details when submitting a report.
  • Do not submit false, malicious, or deliberately misleading reports to target another user.
  • Do not organize mass-reporting campaigns to silence people simply because you disagree with them.
  • Avoid publicly sharing private information about the person you report.
  • Allow administrators time to review reports and take appropriate action.
⚖️

8. Violations and Account Enforcement

Actions should reflect the nature and seriousness of a violation.

Depending on the circumstances, the platform may take action when content or behavior violates these guidelines.

  • Remove or restrict content that violates applicable platform rules.
  • Issue a warning or request that the user correct their behavior.
  • Temporarily restrict selected features or account access.
  • Suspend or permanently disable accounts for serious or repeated violations.
  • Escalate credible threats or other serious matters where required or appropriate under applicable law.
Fair enforcement: The response should consider the seriousness of the conduct, its impact, relevant context, and repeated violations. Where available, users should have a way to raise concerns about enforcement decisions.

Let's make VoxHub better together 💜

Every post, comment, and interaction helps shape our community. Be thoughtful, respect others, and speak up when something is wrong. A safer platform starts with all of us.
